Privacy Policy

Pacific Ridge Digital LLC respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information through our website, software applications, and related services.

Your Privacy

How We Handle Your Information

Last updated: November 4, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes the privacy practices of Pacific Ridge Digital LLC, an Oregon-based software development and digital services company.

It applies to our website at pacificridgedigital.com, our software applications, and services operated by Pacific Ridge Digital LLC.

Different products may use different technologies. Where a product requires additional privacy disclosures, those disclosures will be provided with the applicable product or service.

2. Information We Collect Through Our Website

When you contact Pacific Ridge Digital LLC, submit a project inquiry, or complete a lead form, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide.

This information may include your name, email address, telephone number, business or organization name, requested services, project budget, project timeline, and other information you choose to provide.

3. Software Application Privacy

Pacific Ridge Digital LLC develops software applications for desktop, mobile, and potentially web-based platforms.

Our locally operated desktop applications are designed to perform their core processing functions on the user's device. These functions do not require uploading the user's files to Pacific Ridge Digital LLC.

Applications that use online accounts, cloud storage, remote processing, subscriptions, or other connected services may require additional information. We will disclose applicable data practices before introducing those features.

4. Local File Processing

Certain Pacific Ridge Digital applications allow users to select, create, modify, organize, or process files stored on their computers.

Pacific Ridge STEM Studio processes selected audio files locally on supported desktop systems. Audio separation and output-file generation do not require transmitting the selected audio files to Pacific Ridge Digital LLC.

Pacific Ridge File Studio performs file management operations locally, including duplicate detection, organization, renaming, copying, moving, and deletion. These operations do not require uploading the selected files to Pacific Ridge Digital LLC.

Pacific Ridge PDF Studio provides document-processing functionality. The privacy practices of each released version will reflect its implemented features and any applicable connected services.

Files processed locally remain under the user's control, subject to the operating system's permissions and the actions selected by the user.

5. Mobile and Cloud-Based Applications

Pacific Ridge Digital LLC may develop mobile applications, web applications, and subscription-based software services.

Some applications may require internet connectivity, user accounts, remote processing, or cloud-based storage.

If an application collects or transmits personal information, its relevant privacy practices will be disclosed through this policy or a product-specific privacy notice.

6. How We Use Information

Information provided to Pacific Ridge Digital LLC may be used to respond to inquiries, communicate with customers, provide requested services, manage business relationships, and fulfill applicable legal obligations.

Where online software services are offered, information may also be processed as necessary to provide the specific functionality requested by users.

7. Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website and services used with our website may use cookies or similar technologies to provide functionality, measure website activity, and understand how visitors interact with our website.

Third-party providers may process technical information such as browser type, device information, pages visited, and general usage information according to their own privacy policies.

Website analytics practices are separate from the local file-processing features of our desktop applications.

8. Advertising and Lead Forms

Pacific Ridge Digital LLC may advertise its products and services through third-party platforms, including Facebook and Instagram.

Information submitted through advertising lead forms may be processed by the advertising platform according to its privacy policy.

Information received by Pacific Ridge Digital LLC through these forms is used to respond to inquiries and communicate about requested services.

9. App Stores and Third-Party Services

Our software may be distributed through third-party marketplaces, including the Microsoft Store, Apple App Store, and Google Play.

These marketplaces may process purchase information, payment details, account information, and transaction records according to their own privacy policies.

Pacific Ridge Digital LLC may receive sales reports, transaction information, or other information made available by these marketplaces.

We may also use third-party providers for website hosting, communications, payment processing, and business operations.

10. Information Sharing

Pacific Ridge Digital LLC does not sell personal information.

We may share information with service providers when reasonably necessary to operate our website, respond to inquiries, provide requested services, or conduct legitimate business operations.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, property, or security of Pacific Ridge Digital LLC or others.

11. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information entrusted to Pacific Ridge Digital LLC.

No electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should maintain appropriate backups and device security measures when working with locally stored files.

12. Data Retention

We retain information collected through our business operations for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, provide services, maintain business records, and meet applicable legal or administrative requirements.

Files processed locally by desktop applications are not retained by Pacific Ridge Digital LLC solely because a user processes them through those applications.

13. Your Privacy Choices

You may contact Pacific Ridge Digital LLC to inquire about personal information you have provided to us or to request correction or deletion where applicable.

Certain information may need to be retained to meet legal, financial, or administrative obligations.

14. Children's Privacy

Our website and general-purpose software applications are not specifically directed toward children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 through our general-purpose software applications.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the matter.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Pacific Ridge Digital LLC may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our software, services, business practices, or applicable requirements.

Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised date.
