Privacy Policy

Pacific Ridge Digital respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information you provide when contacting us or using our website.

Your Privacy

How We Handle Your Information

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Information We Collect

Pacific Ridge Digital LLC may collect information that you voluntarily provide when you contact us, submit a project inquiry, or complete a lead form.

This information may include your name, email address, phone number, business or organization name, information about the services you are interested in, project budget, project timeline, and other details you choose to provide about your project.

How We Use Your Information

Information you provide is used to respond to your inquiry, communicate with you about your project, understand your business needs, provide requested information about our services, and manage our relationship with prospective and existing clients.

Pacific Ridge Digital LLC does not sell your personal information.

Website and Lead Forms

Information may be submitted through forms on the Pacific Ridge Digital website or through advertising platforms such as Meta.

Website form submissions may be processed by third-party services that help us receive and manage inquiries. Those providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Advertising

Pacific Ridge Digital LLC may use online advertising platforms, including Meta platforms such as Facebook and Instagram, to promote our services.

When you submit information through an advertising lead form, the advertising platform may also process that information according to its own privacy policy. Information received by Pacific Ridge Digital will be used to respond to your inquiry and communicate with you about the services you requested.

Cookies and Analytics

Our website and third-party services used with our website may use cookies or similar technologies to provide functionality, measure website activity, and understand how visitors interact with our website.

Third-party services may collect technical information such as browser type, device information, pages visited, and general usage information according to their own privacy policies.

Sharing of Information

Pacific Ridge Digital LLC does not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when reasonably necessary to operate our website, process inquiries, communicate with clients, or provide requested services.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, property, or security of Pacific Ridge Digital LLC or others.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information submitted to us. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, provide services, maintain business records, and meet applicable legal or administrative requirements.

Your Choices

You may contact Pacific Ridge Digital LLC to ask about personal information you have provided to us or to request that we update or delete information when appropriate.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

Pacific Ridge Digital LLC may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
